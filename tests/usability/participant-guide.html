CampusRSO — Participant Guide

Usability test & task sheet for the Campus Resource Sharing Platform
BETA TEST About 15 minutes
Laptop or phone
Version 1.0

What you are testing

CampusRSO is a booking platform for shared university facilities. Each faculty runs its own halls, labs and equipment, students lend their own things to each other for tokens, and the system sorts out clashes and approvals.

We are not testing you — we are testing whether the interface makes sense first time round. Where you get stuck or annoyed is exactly what we need to hear.

Step 1

Create an account

Sign up with your faculty code and verify your email.

Step 2

Work through the tasks

Do them in order, at your normal pace. Skip any that will not work for you.

Step 3

Fill the feedback form

Ten quick statements plus a few open questions. Two minutes.

Finding your way around

Everything runs off the left sidebar on a laptop, or the bar along the bottom on a phone.

Dashboard — your stats and shortcuts
Notifications — approvals and reminders
Resources — halls, labs and equipment
Profile — your details and photo
ST Resource — items students lend out
Token count — top bar, students only
ST Borrows — what you borrowed and lent
Chat bubble — bottom right, ask it anything
Bookings — yours, sorted by status

Before you start

  • Only edit or delete things you created. Other testers share your faculty.
  • Keep booking titles and item descriptions free of anything private — others see them.
  • If something errors, note what you clicked and move on to the next task.
  • Do not ask for help. Not finding something is a result, not a failure.

Your tasks

Work down the list in order. Each task says what to do and how to tell you are finished. Do not worry about getting it wrong — where you hesitate is useful information.

Part A · The basics

Everyone
1

Get your bearings. Sign in and look over the dashboard — the number cards and the Recent Bookings list.

Done when you can say what the dashboard is telling you.

2

Find a specific resource. In Resources, find a hall or lab that holds at least 30 people. Try the search box.

Done when you have one open on screen.

3

Check when it is free. On that page, scroll to Today's Schedule and see what is already booked.

Done when you know whether it is free this afternoon.

4

Book it. Click New Booking and work the three steps — resource, date and time, then a title and headcount.

Done when the confirmation appears and the booking is listed as Pending.

5

Try to double-book. Now book the same resource again for the same time slot. The system should stop you.

Done when you see the clash warning. Note whether it made sense to you.

6

Find your booking and cancel it. In Bookings, use the status tabs to find the one you made, open it and cancel it.

Done when its status reads Cancelled.

7

Check your notifications. Open Notifications, read what is there and click Mark All Read.

Done when the unread badge is gone.

8

Update your profile. In Profile, change your mobile number and upload a photo. It will ask you to crop it.

Done when your new photo shows in the top bar.

9

Ask the assistant something. Open the chat bubble, bottom right, and ask it a real question. Sinhala works.

Done when you have read its answer and formed an opinion of it.

Part B · Lending between students

Students only — skip if you signed up as staff
10

Check your token balance. Find the token count in the top bar. Tokens are what you spend to borrow things.

Done when you know how many you have.

11

Put one of your own items up. In ST Resource, click Share My Item. Set a condition and an hourly token cost.

Done when it shows up under the My Items tab.

12

Borrow something from someone else. Under All Items, request one that is not yours. Check the token cost before confirming.

Done when the request is in and you have seen what it will cost.

13

Look at both sides of a loan. In ST Borrows, switch between My Borrows and Lent Out. Approve a request, mark one returned.

Done when you can tell the two tabs apart without thinking.

Part C · Running a faculty

Faculty admin testers only

Only do this part if the team asked you to. Everyone else stops after Part B and goes straight to the form.

14

Add a resource. From Resources, add a lab or piece of equipment with a name, location and capacity.

Done when it appears in the list for everyone in your faculty.

15

Clear the approval queue. In Bookings, filter to Pending. Approve one, reject another with a reason.

Done when both have moved out of the pending tab.

16

Edit a resource. Open any resource you own and change its capacity or description.

Done when the change shows on the resource page.

17

Look at your users. Open Users under Admin. Do not change roles or suspend anyone unless asked to.

Done when you have seen who is in your faculty and what role they hold.

If you get stuck

No verification email Check spam. On the verify screen, click Resend. Gmail is the most reliable.
Faculty code rejected It is case sensitive. Copy it from this sheet rather than typing it.
No resources listed You may be in a faculty with nothing set up yet. Tell the team and stop there.
Not enough tokens Pick a cheaper item or a shorter slot. Note it in the form — it is useful to know.
A page will not load Refresh once. If it still fails, note the page and carry on.
